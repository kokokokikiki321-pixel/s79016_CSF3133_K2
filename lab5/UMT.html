Welcome to UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It focuses on education, research and innovation, particularly in marine science, aquatic resources and environmental studies.

About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu provides higher education opportunities in various academic fields. The university aims to produce knowledgeable and skilled graduates who can contribute to society and the development of the country.

UMT also emphasizes research and development in marine science, fisheries, oceanography and environmental sustainability.
