About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu Food Festival is an exciting event that celebrates the traditional food and culture of Terengganu. Visitors can explore different local dishes and enjoy the unique flavours of Malaysian cuisine.

The festival brings together local food vendors and visitors in one place. It is a great opportunity to support local businesses, discover traditional recipes and enjoy delicious food with family and friends.
